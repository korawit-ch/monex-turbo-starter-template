# Decisions

Decisions a later change must respect. Newest first. Rewrite an entry when it changes, and update its date.

Entry format:

```markdown
## <YYYY-MM-DD> <short title>

- **Decision:** what was decided.
- **Why:** the reason, including the options that were rejected.
- **Source:** ticket, meeting or person.
- **Affects:** workspaces, contracts or screens that must follow it.
```
